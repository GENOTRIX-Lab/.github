### 🔬 About Our Lab
**GENOTRIX Lab** is an independent bioinformatics and genomic research hub dedicated to developing reproducible computational workflows, sequence analysis tools, and multi-omics data pipelines.

---

### 🚀 Research Focus & Capabilities
* 🧬 **Molecular Genetics & Phylogenetics**: High-resolution sequence alignment, molecular barcoding, and evolutionary tree reconstruction.
* 💻 **Pipeline Engineering**: Custom executable scripts in R and Python tailored for peer-reviewed publication data.
* 📊 **Comparative Genomics**: Structural gene family analysis and pairwise distance profiling.
